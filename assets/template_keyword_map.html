Before Reading 读前热身

Talk first, then guess from the title! 先聊自己，再从标题猜故事 ✏️

❄️➡️🌸

Winter is long, long, long. Do you want to sleep or play outside?

😴

Title: "Spring." Toad is still asleep! How can Frog wake him up? Guess!

🔍 Read with this question: Why did Frog tear the calendar to May?

Vocabulary 故事词汇 + 固定搭配

Choose a set. Learn the story words, then meet each useful chunk in a real situation. 选择一组：先学故事词汇，再用自然的小情境理解整块语言。

01

spring

EN: the season after winter.

中文：春天

Example: It is spring now.

02

wake up

EN: stop sleeping.

中文：醒来

Example: Frog told Toad to wake up.

03

sleepy

EN: ready or wanting to sleep.

中文：困倦的

Example: Toad felt sleepy.

04

clear

EN: bright and easy to see through.

中文：清澈的、明亮的

Example: The morning was clear.

05

April

EN: the fourth month of the year.

中文：四月

Example: April comes in spring.

06

whole

EN: all of something.

中文：整个的

Example: They spent the whole day together.

07

together

EN: with each other.

中文：一起

Example: Frog and Toad went outside together.

08

calendar

EN: a chart that shows days and months.

中文：日历

Example: Frog looked at the calendar.

09

tore off

EN: pulled something away by tearing.

中文：撕掉

Example: He tore off a piece of paper.

10

lonely

EN: sad because you are alone.

中文：孤单的

Example: Frog did not want to feel lonely.

run up the path to someone's house

STORY

沿着小路跑向某人的家。

Frog ran up the path to Toad's house.

Try it: I see my friend at the door. I run up the path to her house.

knock on the front door

CORE

敲前门。

He knocked on the front door.

Try it: I knock on the front door and wait for Grandma.

there was no answer

CORE

没有人回应。

There was no answer.

Try it: I call Ben's name, but there is no answer. He may be outside.

pull all the covers over your head

STORY

把所有被子拉过头顶，把自己蒙起来。

He had pulled all the covers over his head.

Try it: The room is cold. I pull all the covers over my head.

a whole new year together

CORE

一起度过完整的新一年。

“And it means that we can begin a whole new year together, Toad.”

Try it: School begins today. My friends and I can have a whole new year together.

count the stars

CORE

数星星。

“In the evenings we will sit right here on this front porch and count the stars.”

Try it: The sky is clear tonight. We sit outside and count the stars.

go back to bed

CORE

回到床上继续睡觉或休息。

“I am going back to bed.”

Try it: I still feel sick, so I go back to bed and rest.

at about half past May

STORY

大约在五月过了一半的时候，是故事里的幽默说法。

“Come back again and wake me up at about half past May.”

Try it: Toad wants to sleep for many more weeks. He says, “Wake me at about half past May.”

🌸 And off they went, out the door — into the spring world, together at last. Frog "撕"来了五月——因为和朋友在一起的春天，一天都不想等。

Characters 人物 · 用证据说性格

Sentence frame: "___ is ___ because ___." 句型支架

🐸 Toad 蟾蜍

The sleepy-head — he wants to sleep half the spring away! 大瞌睡虫，想把半个春天都睡掉

Toad is sleepy because he has slept all winter long.

🐸 Frog 青蛙

He cannot wait — he tore the calendar all the way to May! 一天都等不了，直接把日历撕到五月

Frog is clever because he made the calendar say "May."
