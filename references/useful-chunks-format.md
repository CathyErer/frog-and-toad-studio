# Useful Chunks Format

Use this format for every Frog and Toad story's Vocabulary tab.

## Required set

- Keep the existing 10 Story Words.
- Add exactly 8 Useful Chunks: 5 `CORE` expressions that students can reuse often and 3 `STORY` expressions that are especially useful for understanding or retelling this story.
- Select chunks from the complete story, not only from the current Story Map.

## Required fields

Each chunk has four visible parts:

1. the phrase or reusable sentence frame;
2. a short Chinese explanation in `.zh`;
3. one source-verified sentence from the story, displayed directly with no `In the story:` label;
4. `Try it:` followed by a natural two- or three-sentence mini-context that makes the meaning clear.

Do not add `How to use`. Do not force every mini-context into `That means ...`; vary the explanation naturally. Keep nouns and pronouns consistent, for example:

> I'm looking for my pencil. I don't know where it is, and I want to find it.

## Obsidian source shape

```markdown
**Useful Chunks（8 个固定搭配）：**

1. **look for something** — CORE
   - 中文：寻找某个东西。
   - They began to look for the button in the tall grass.
   - Try it: I'm looking for my pencil. I don't know where it is, and I want to find it.
```

The sync script reads this block. Keep the four lines for each numbered item in this order.

## Verification

- Verify every source sentence against the scanned book page.
- Require 10 Story Word cards, 8 Useful Chunk cards, 5 CORE labels, and 3 STORY labels.
- Test Story Words / Useful Chunks switching and EN only / 中英 switching.
- Reject any visible `In the story:`, `How to use`, or mechanical repeated `That means` wording.
